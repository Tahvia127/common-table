Give

A dollar buys more here than it does at the store

We buy at wholesale and take surplus at no cost, so a gift stretches further than a grocery run. Our current cost is $0.42 per meal.

Demo only. In production this hands off to Zeffy, which charges nonprofits nothing, so 100% of a gift arrives. Donorbox and Givebutter drop in the same way.

Give monthly

Recurring gifts let us commit to wholesalers months ahead, which is where the price per meal actually drops.

$20 a month is 571 meals over a year.

Programs

Three things, done properly

Neighborhood pantries

Twelve sites, open the same hours every week. No appointment, no means test, no questions about why you came.

Surplus rescue

We collect from wholesalers and farms the day before food would be thrown out, and it reaches a pantry within eighteen hours.

Saturday delivery

For households that cannot travel, volunteers run boxes to the door. Currently 340 households a week.
