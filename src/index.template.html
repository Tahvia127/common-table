Programs

Three things, done properly

Neighborhood pantries

Twelve sites, open the same hours every week. No appointment, no means test, no questions about why you came.

Surplus rescue

We collect from wholesalers and farms the day before food would be thrown out, and it reaches a pantry within eighteen hours.

Saturday delivery

For households that cannot travel, volunteers run boxes to the door. Currently 340 households a week.
